Campaign Brief: Q3 Postpaid Winback

Portfolio: Postpaid Winback
Purpose: Winback
Cadence: Monthly
Medium: SMS, Outbound Call

Target Audience:
We want to target high-value customers who have recently lapsed from postpaid.
Specifically, customers who:
- Were previously on a postpaid plan
- Have been out of contract for more than 3 months
- Had an ARPU of $80 or more before lapsing
- Have not been contacted in the last 60 days

Exclusions:
- Do not contact customers who have opted out of SMS and outbound calls
- Exclude customers who responded to a campaign in the last 30 days
- Exclude customers currently in a debt collection process

Offer:
We are offering a loyalty discount of 20% off the first 3 months on any new postpaid plan.
Eligible customers must not currently hold an active postpaid service.

Channel Sequence:
1. SMS on Day 1 (10am AEST)
2. Outbound call on Day 4 if no response to SMS

Success Metric:
Customer re-activates on any postpaid plan within 30 days of first contact.

Notes:
"High-value" for this portfolio means ARPU >= $80 based on last 3 months of usage.
"Recently lapsed" means contract_end_date is more than 90 days ago.
